EduSphere — Accessible & Friendly Learning Management System

HTML CSS JavaScript

Overview & Design Concept

Most existing Learning Management Systems (LMS) feature rigid, complex interfaces that create navigation barriers for novice users. EduSphere was designed as an inclusive, general-purpose LMS solution tailored to be creative, engaging, and highly intuitive across various educational levels.

Visual Identity & Color Strategy

Soft Blue was selected as the primary visual identity based on color psychology principles that promote calm, reduce study fatigue, and maintain visual neutrality across age groups. Clean sans-serif typography and minimal layouts were chosen for high readability, ensuring an effortless and comfortable reading experience for students of all levels.

Key Features

  • AI Chatbot Assistant: Provides instant responses for general inquiries and technical troubleshooting, eliminating the need to wait for manual teacher replies or physical school visits.
  • Simplified Schedule Layout: Simplifies class schedule visualization using structured color-coded cards, making daily agendas effortless to understand even for elementary school students.
