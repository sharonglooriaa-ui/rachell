Overview & Problem Statement

VirtuPlan is a mobile application concept designed to address time management challenges and productivity decline frequently experienced by students and young professionals. The platform integrates task management, calendar scheduling, and priority reminders into a single, clean visual ecosystem.

UI/UX Focus & Cognitive Load Reduction

Developed as an interactive prototype in Figma, VirtuPlan prioritizes navigation efficiency to minimize cognitive load. Through intuitive bottom navigation, urgency-based color coding, and flexible calendar layouts, users can organize their schedules effortlessly without feeling overwhelmed by complex UI elements.

Key Design Highlights

  • Smart Task Categorization: Clear visual distinction between urgent deadlines, daily routines, and long-term goals.
  • Seamless Calendar Integration: Unified daily and weekly views for frictionless agenda planning.
  • Minimalist Interface: Clean aesthetics with high visual hierarchy to maintain focus and user engagement.
