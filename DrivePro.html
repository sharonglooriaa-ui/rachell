DrivePro — Vehicle Service & Queue Management System

HTML CSS JavaScript
🔗 Live Web Application

Problem Identified

Conventional vehicle workshop operations often suffer from manual queue handling and disconnected service tracking, creating operational bottlenecks for both customers and workshop staff:

  • Uncertain Waiting & Service Duration: Customers lacked real-time visibility into their queue position, active bay assignment, and estimated completion time.
  • Unstructured Bay & Queue Allocation: Manual coordination between front-desk intake and workshop bays led to idle bays or delayed vehicle call-ups.
  • Skill-Mismatch Risks for Hybrid & EV Units: Assigning mechanics without filtering certifications for Gasoline, Hybrid, and Electric Vehicles risked operational delays and safety issues.
  • Manual Cost & Sparepart Recap: On-the-spot service adjustments and sparepart replacements were prone to calculation errors before final billing.

System Solution & Project Scope

To streamline end-to-end workshop operations, a web-based service and queue management system was developed with four core functional modules:

  • Service Booking & Walk-In Intake: Supports online reservations as well as rapid front-desk ticket generation for both registered members and walk-in guests, complete with engine-specific service catalogs and automated cost & duration estimation.
  • Real-Time Queue & Bay Monitoring: Provides a live branch-wide bay status board (Empty, Called, In-Progress) alongside a dedicated active-bay workspace for staff to manage job progression and dynamically adjust remaining service time.
  • Skill-Based Mechanic Allocation: Automatically filters available mechanics based on vehicle engine type (Gasoline vs. certified Hybrid/EV specialists) and real-time bay availability when assigning waiting tickets to empty bays.
  • Dynamic Service & Sparepart Billing Recap: Enables staff to update service checklists, log sparepart replacements with installation fees, and archive completed jobs into a searchable service history log.

Project Deliverables & Business Impact (Output)

The implementation of DrivePro established a structured, transparent, and responsive workflow across customer and workshop touchpoints:

  • Transparent Customer Queue Experience: Customers can track queue tickets, bay assignments, and live status updates without needing to repeatedly inquire at the service counter.
  • Optimized Bay Utilization & Faster Turnaround: Clear visibility of empty bays and waiting queues accelerates vehicle call-ups and minimizes workshop idle time.
  • Accurate Technician Routing: Skill-based filtering ensures high-voltage Hybrid and EV vehicles are strictly handled by certified specialists.
  • Reliable Audit Trail & Cost Transparency: Every completed service records exact timestamps, assigned personnel, itemized services, and sparepart costs for accurate handoff to cashier checkout.
