Overview & Gamification Concept

Developing public speaking and everyday communication skills is often intimidating due to stage fright and self-doubt. ELO was designed as a mobile application concept utilizing gamification principles to transform speech practice into a structured, highly engaging, and interactive journey.

User Experience & Challenge Mechanics

The core mechanic revolves around daily communication challenges structured like a video game. Users practice speaking tasks, receive performance feedback, and unlock higher levels with progressively increasing difficulty to continuously build confidence over time.

Figma Design & Asset Scope

This project represents a comprehensive end-to-end UI/UX study created entirely within Figma:

  • Brand Identity: Designing the custom ELO logo, visual theme, and color palette.
  • Custom Assets: Crafting unique vector icons and visual assets tailored for gamified progress tracking.
  • High-Fidelity Screens: Designing complete user flows from onboarding, challenge dashboards, to level progression screens.
