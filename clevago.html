Clevago — Enterprise Back-Office & Vendor Management System

Figma

Overview & Operational Context

Clevago is a case study centered on a mid-sized laundry enterprise requiring back-office operational efficiency. This project focused on designing an internal web interface to optimize vendor coordination, asset maintenance, supply chain tracking, and storage rack management.

System Modules & Core Capabilities

  • Vendor Management Directory: A centralized database to record and categorize vendor data (machine repair technicians, detergent suppliers, and specialized footwear/bag outsourcing partners) for rapid coordination.
  • Smart Inventory Tracking: Real-time supply tracking with automated low-stock alerts for essential laundry supplies.
  • Asset & Machine Maintenance: A dashboard for monitoring laundry equipment status, logging repair histories, and scheduling routine machine maintenance.
  • Order & Storage Rack Tracking: Real-time tracking of customer items mapped across storage racks based on order fulfillment stages.
