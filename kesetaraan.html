Disability Equality & Inclusivity Cheerful Infographic

Canva

Overview & Visual Concept

Awareness campaigns surrounding disability equality are often presented using overly somber visual approaches. This infographic poster takes an alternate approach by employing a cheerful visual style dominated by a vibrant pink color palette to engage audience attention effectively.

Objective & Visual Messaging

The warm color scheme and approachable graphic elements aim to foster a positive perspective on public inclusivity. The poster educates the public that accessible facilities are a fundamental human right and a celebration of social equality. Information is presented using a clear visual hierarchy with scannable text, structured data blocks, and supportive concept icons for optimal readability.
