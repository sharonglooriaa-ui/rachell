Filmint — Cinema Reviews & Ticket Reservation Web Platform

Figma
🔗 FilmInt Porto

Overview & Streaming Concept

Filmint is an end-to-end web streaming platform designed to deliver a complete entertainment experience. Built with a user-centric approach, Filmint covers the entire user journey—from account onboarding, flexible subscription management, and direct film playback to personalized viewing histories and community reviews.

Core Features & Platform Capabilities

  • User Onboarding & Authentication: Intuitive Sign Up and Sign In forms for secure profile creation and access management.
  • Subscription & Gift Plans: Flexible membership plan choices and a dedicated gift feature for sharing subscriptions with other users.
  • Interactive Film Catalog & Playback: Seamless movie browsing with categorized genres and an embedded video player for instant online viewing.
  • Viewing History & Reviews: Personalized watch history logs alongside a community rating and review system to share movie feedback.
