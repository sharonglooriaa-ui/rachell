HIMA IS — Social Media Content Creation & Photography

Event Photography Photo Editing Canva Social Media Management

Overview & Role

As part of the Information Systems Student Association (HIMASIF) media team, this role involved end-to-end planning and execution of social media content to strengthen organizational brand image and boost audience engagement across official channels, particularly Instagram.

Execution & Creative Scope

The project encompassed a complete creative workflow from concept to final publication:

  • Content Strategy & Ideation: Planning publishing schedules and conceptualizing engaging visual formats for departmental events and official announcements.
  • Event Photography: On-site photography capturing key moments, speakers, and student activities during official organizational events.
  • Photo Editing: Editing and retouching photographs to maintain visual harmony and brand consistency throughout the feed.
  • Layout & Poster Design: Designing clean, informative poster graphics and Instagram carousel layouts tailored for quick scanning and high engagement.
