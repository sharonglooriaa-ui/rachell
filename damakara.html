Damakara — Integrated Fashion Enterprise System Solution

Figma HTML CSS JavaScript

Problem Identified

Operational workflows were constrained by fragmented digital tools and manual processes, leading to critical operational bottlenecks:

  • High Risk of Human Error: Manual calculations for fabric needs and reliance on physical spreadsheets across production, warehouse, and finance teams.
  • Data Silos & Fragmented Communication: Disconnected stock monitoring between online and offline channels managed by separate PICs, relying heavily on unorganized WhatsApp threads.
  • Lack of Automated Stock Alerts: No system notifications for low-stock thresholds or stagnant inventory (dead stock), leading to stockouts during critical moments.
  • Inefficient Warehouse Operations: Absence of systematic rack mapping forced warehouse staff to locate items manually from memory.

System Solution & Project Scope

To address these bottlenecks, an integrated web-based system architecture was developed, comprising four core modules:

  • Planning & Production Module: Bridges design and production teams by automating Bill of Materials (BOM) fabric calculations, managing custom orders, issuing digital Work Orders (WO) to vendors, and streamlining Quality Control (QC).
  • Warehouse Management System (WMS): Streamlines stock movement through barcode scanning, automated rack location assignment (putaway), guided picking lists, and real-time omnichannel stock synchronization with Jubelio.
  • Store Management System (POS): Manages daily retail transactions, POS checkouts, store returns, discount events, and automates digital restock requests sent directly to the central warehouse.
  • Executive Dashboard: Serves as a centralized management hub featuring real-time sales analytics, cumulative inventory visibility, and automated alerts for low-stock and dead-stock items.

Project Deliverables & Business Impact (Output)

The realization of the web platform delivered tangible improvements across Dama Kara's operational lifecycle:

  • Operational Efficiency & Data Accuracy: Eliminated spreadsheet dependency, reducing human error in raw material planning and financial recap processes.
  • Accelerated Warehouse Distribution: Digital rack coordinates drastically reduced item retrieval time for both online orders and store transfers.
  • Real-Time Inventory & POS Integration: Automatic stock deductions at retail POS and synchronized stock updates across all sales channels prevent over-selling and stockouts.
  • Data-Driven Executive Decision Making: Instant access to real-time sales trends and automated inventory alerts empowers proactive promotional and restock strategies.
