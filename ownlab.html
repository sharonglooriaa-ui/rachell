Overview & Unified Concept

OwnLab is an integrated project combining an agency web platform with an end-to-end digital marketing strategy. Unifying these aspects demonstrates a complete operational understanding of creative agency workflows, from client onboarding to automated publishing and promotional campaigns.

Project Scope & Core Features

  • Agency Web Platform: Designed as a collaborative hub for clients and the OwnLab team. Features include content idea submissions, production shoot scheduling, video editing review workflows, and social media content scheduling.
  • Campaign & Advertising Strategy: Creation of digital ad concepts (video and graphic assets) formulated to highlight OwnLab's value proposition to potential business partners seeking digital marketing solutions.
